A request, not a campaign

Bryan Rolfe is 34. He's a father of two. His liver is failing.

Doctors have told him a transplant is his only real chance. He's not famous. He's not selling anything. He's just asking — and so are the people who love him.

In his own words Bryan Rolfe

"I'm fighting for my life and I need your help. My liver is failing, and without a transplant, I won't survive much longer.

I've been dealing with constant pain, extreme fatigue, and my body's slowly shutting down. The doctors say a transplant is my only real chance.

The medical costs are overwhelming — surgery, medications, hospital stays, and travel for treatment. I'm asking for help to cover what insurance won't. Every donation brings me one step closer to getting the new liver I need to keep living."
